PROBLEM STATEMENT + ANSWERS (BUILD-READY BRIEF)
Landing Page Assignment: Communicate Value, Structure a Funnel, Drive Action
=============================================================================
Chosen offering: CONTENT CREATOR KIT - "Make Your First 3 Reels in 7 Days"
Build tool: Antigravity (single-page landing page, mobile-first)


-----------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. PROBLEM STATEMENT
-----------------------------------------------------------------------------
A first-time visitor lands on a page knowing little about the offering. They
are curious at best, skeptical at worst, and one click from leaving. The
challenge is to turn that curiosity into understanding, and understanding
into action, using clear, simple, relatable communication (not design polish).

The page must explain why the visitor should care, answer their doubts, build
trust, and make the call to action feel like the obvious next step.

Problem for the target user: Many beginners want to start posting Reels but
never do. They don't know what to film, feel awkward on camera, think they
need gear and editing skills, and get stuck planning instead of posting.

Goal: Demonstrate the ability to communicate value, structure a simple funnel,
and guide a user to take action.


-----------------------------------------------------------------------------
2. THE ASSIGNMENT QUESTIONS, ANSWERED
-----------------------------------------------------------------------------

Q1. Who is the target user?
Beginners aged 18-30 (students, early-career professionals, side-hustlers,
small business owners) who want to start creating Reels but have never posted
consistently. They own a phone, not a camera. Their blocker is not lack of
interest, it is not knowing where to start.

Q2. What action do we want them to take?
Click the single CTA and sign up (name + email) to get the Content Creator Kit
and start the 7-day plan. One page, one action.

Q3. Why should they care? (the value)
In 7 days, with only a phone, they go from "I don't know what to post" to 3
finished Reels, using ready-made ideas, scripts, shot lists and a day-by-day
plan. No guesswork, no gear, no editing experience needed.

Q4. What doubts will a first-time visitor have, and how do we remove them?
- "I'm not good on camera."  -> Scripts and simple formats, including ones
  with no face on camera.
- "I don't have equipment."  -> Everything is shot on a phone, with natural
  light.
- "I don't know how to edit." -> Beginner editing steps using free apps.
- "I don't have time."       -> About 30-45 minutes a day for 7 days.
- "What if it doesn't work for my niche?" -> Templates are adaptable to any
  niche (fitness, food, business, fashion, study, etc.).
- "What do I actually get?"  -> Clear list of what is inside the kit.
- "Is it worth it / what does it cost?" -> State price or "free" clearly;
  add a simple guarantee or "no card needed" line.

Q5. How do we build interest and trust?
- Relatable opening line that names their exact struggle.
- Plain language, no jargon.
- Concrete outcomes (3 Reels, 7 days) instead of vague promises.
- Transparent list of what's inside and a sample peek.
- Social proof: testimonials, creator results or beta-user quotes. Use real
  ones only; keep clearly marked placeholders until real ones exist.
- Risk reducer: free / no card / easy opt-out.

Q6. How do we guide curiosity -> understanding -> action?
See the page flow in Section 3. Each section answers the question the visitor
has at that moment and ends by pointing them toward the CTA.

Q7. How do we make the value obvious and the CTA natural?
- Headline states the outcome and timeframe.
- Same CTA text repeated at the top, middle and bottom of the page.
- CTA copy describes the benefit ("Start My 7-Day Plan"), not "Submit".
- One primary button color; no competing links or distractions.

3. PAGE STRUCTURE AND COPY (THE FUNNEL)
-----------------------------------------------------------------------------

SECTION 1 - HERO (Curiosity)
Headline:    Make Your First 3 Reels in 7 Days.
Subheadline: Just your phone and 30 minutes a day. No experience, no fancy
             gear, no overthinking.
CTA button:  Start My 7-Day Plan
Microcopy:   Free to start. No credit card needed. [adjust to real pricing]

SECTION 2 - THE PROBLEM (Relatability)
Heading: Sound familiar?
- You've been "meaning to start" posting for weeks.
- You don't know what to film or what to say.
- Editing looks complicated and everyone else seems to know what they're doing.
Closer: You don't need more motivation. You need a simple plan.

SECTION 3 - THE SOLUTION (Understanding)
Heading: Meet the Content Creator Kit
Text: A day-by-day plan that takes you from zero to 3 finished Reels in one
      week. Follow it step by step and post.

SECTION 4 - WHAT'S INSIDE (Value)
- 7-day action plan (what to do each day)
- 15+ Reel ideas for any niche
- Ready-to-use hook and caption templates
- Simple shot lists (what to film, how long, from what angle)
- Beginner editing guide using free apps
- Posting checklist
Mid-page CTA: Get the Kit

SECTION 5 - HOW IT WORKS (Clarity)
Step 1: Sign up and get the kit.
Step 2: Follow one small task per day.
Step 3: Post your 3 Reels by Day 7.

SECTION 6 - WHO IT'S FOR (Self-qualification)
For you if: you're a beginner, you have a phone, you want to start posting.
Not for you if: you want overnight virality or advanced editing tutorials.

SECTION 7 - TRUST (Proof)
- 2-3 short testimonials (use real ones; placeholders until then)
- Optional: a sample page from the kit, or a before/after of a first Reel

SECTION 8 - FAQ (Remove doubts)
- Do I need a camera? No, a phone is enough.
- What if I'm shy on camera? There are no-face formats and simple scripts.
- How much time per day? About 30-45 minutes.
- Will it work for my niche? Yes, the templates are adaptable.
- What does it cost? [state clearly]

SECTION 9 - FINAL CTA (Action)
Heading: Your first Reel is closer than you think.
Text:    Start today and have 3 Reels live by next week.
CTA button: Start My 7-Day Plan
Microcopy:  Free to start. Takes less than a minute.
